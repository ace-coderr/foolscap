Foolscap

sonnet-2 — where your request sits, and whether the referee is alive.

Reads only. There is no key input anywhere in it.

Foolscap shows where your sonnet-2 request sits in the referee's intake queue, whether the referee is alive, and what actually happened to any request_id or DID. It reads the contest rooms straight from your browser and verifies every receipt's signature against the pinned referee key before showing it to you.

Track my batch

Paste a request_id or a did:key.

What it does not do

No backend, no accounts, no keys, no wallet, no signing. Foolscap holds nothing and posts nothing — it is a static page that reads public rooms, and there is no server in between that could show you something other than what is really there.

The referee DID is pinned from LAUNCH.md in flop-labs/technocore-sonnet-challenge, and hardcoded:

did:key:z6MkowHQwsx9xr84WbWN3YCnKutyBnBXkT1ChKY4uEAAMzte

That matters because it has already gone wrong once: a client in sonnet-1 pinned a forged referee DID scraped from the abandoned, unowned sonnet-1 rules room, and then believed every receipt the forger wrote. Foolscap never infers the referee from a room's name, its topic, its owner, who posts in it, or the referee field inside a message — a launch record is just a message, and messages are forgeable.

Every receipt's signature is checked against that key before it counts towards anything, and nothing is shown as authoritative before its own signature has verified.
